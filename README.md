## 🌦️ Game Description
Red is exploring uncharted territory with their friends Orange, Green, and Pink until a major, unexpected storm causes the friend group to scatter. As the leader of the group, Red needs to maneuver through obstacles and avoid enemies to find their friends and reunite the friend group, so they may be together again!

#### 🍎 Red can...
- Walk around to explore the terrain
- Jump and dash to complete long jumps between platforms and avoid enemies
- Collect items, like coins and extra lives
- Lose a life by falling off the platforms or getting hit by enemies
- Respawn at last visited checkpoint
- Reach the end of a level to meet up with one of their friends

#### 🍊☘️🌸 Friends can...
- Provide tips on how to survive each level
- Provide encouragement to the player
